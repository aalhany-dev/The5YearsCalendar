The Structural Solution

The 5 Years Calendar restructures the solar year into 13 identical months of exactly 28 days (364 days). This creates a permanent, locked grid where dates and days of the week never shift, preserving the unbroken 7-day cycle.

Flawless Astronomical Alignment

To resolve the astronomical deficit without breaking the 7-day cycle, a 14th intercalary month consisting of exactly 7 days is applied via a strict mathematical algorithm:


  • Added every 5 years.
  • Skipped every 50 years.
  • Skipped every 400 years (with a 25-year offset).
